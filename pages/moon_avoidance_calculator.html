Narrowband (Hα/SII) rejects most moonlight by bandpass alone — tolerable close to a gibbous or full moon. OIII picks up more scatter (moonlight carries blue-green content). Broadband L/RGB is most vulnerable, and at a Bortle 6–7 site the moon adds directly on top of an already-elevated background — prioritize narrowband during moon-heavy weeks, save RGB/L for dark-of-moon. These are heuristic starting points, not hard physics — tighten or loosen them against your own gradient/background results over time. If moon altitude is below 0°, it isn't a factor regardless of separation.
